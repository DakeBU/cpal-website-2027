# Submission Tracks

CPAL 2027 is preparing the following opportunities. The descriptions below
state the intended role of each track; binding submission rules will be posted
with the official call.

## Proceedings Track

An archival track for original, unpublished research. CPAL expects to use
OpenReview and double-blind review. Page limits, dual-submission rules,
publication arrangements, author discussion, and presentation requirements
will be confirmed in the final instructions.

## Recent Spotlight Track

A non-archival presentation track for timely research at different stages of
development, including technically credible work in progress and recent work
that can stimulate discussion in the CPAL community. The final eligibility
window, supporting-material format, review criteria, and presentation format
will be announced with the official call.

## Rising Stars Award

The Rising Stars Award is a **separate call for applications**, not a paper
submission or publication track. See the
[Rising Stars]({{ '/rising_stars/' | relative_url }}) page for updates.
