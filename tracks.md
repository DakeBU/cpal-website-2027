# Submission Tracks

CPAL 2027 retains two research submission tracks:

## Proceedings Track
Archival, double-blind research papers using the CPAL template.

## Recent Spotlight Track
A non-archival presentation track for timely work at different stages of development.

Final submission details and links will be published after committee approval.
