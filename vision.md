# The CPAL vision

The technological success of machine learning increasingly rests on finding simple structure in seemingly overwhelming complexity. High-dimensional data may concentrate near low-dimensional sets; large models may organize around a small number of mechanisms; useful representations may expose symmetry, sparsity, hierarchy, modularity, or compositional rules.

CPAL provides a forum where these shared ideas can be studied across traditional disciplinary boundaries, connecting machine learning, signal processing, optimization, statistics, applied mathematics, neuroscience, scientific computing, hardware, and intelligent systems.
