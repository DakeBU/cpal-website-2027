# The CPAL vision

The technological success of machine learning increasingly rests on finding
simple structure in seemingly overwhelming complexity. High-dimensional data
may concentrate near low-dimensional sets; large models may organize around a
small number of mechanisms; useful representations may expose symmetry,
sparsity, hierarchy, modularity, or compositional rules.

CPAL provides a forum where these shared ideas can be studied across
traditional disciplinary boundaries. The conference connects researchers in
machine learning, signal processing, optimization, statistics, applied
mathematics, neuroscience, scientific computing, hardware, and intelligent
systems.

We use **parsimony** broadly but not vaguely: contributions should identify the
structure that makes a learning problem simpler, explain how that structure is
assumed, learned, or emergent, and demonstrate what understanding or capability
it enables.
